# Dog-Robot-Onshape-Assembl
A comprehensive step-by-step guide and documentation for assembling, mating, and creating an exploded view of a 4-servo dog robot in Onshape.

---
I have uploaded the CAD files and parts for the components (main body, servo motors, horns/brushes, and leg linkages) to this repository.


## 🛠️ Components Included
* Main Body: The central chassis frame.
* Servo Motors & Horns (Brushes): The 4 servo actuators and their corresponding attachment horns.
* Leg Linkages: The 4 individual leg assemblies.

---

## 📋 Assembly & Mating Steps

### 1. Main Body & Servo Placement
* Loaded the main body part first into the Onshape assembly and fixed it in place.
* Imported the 4 servo motors (without their horns/brushes) and defined their mounting positions relative to the body.

### 3. Leg Mating & Alignment
* Assembled the legs to the servo horns using a sequence of specific mates to ensure a precise mechanical fit:
  * Cylindrical Mate: Established the rotational axis alignment between the leg and the servo brush.
  * Parallel Mate: Oriented the leg correctly relative to the servo assembly.
  * Planar Mate: Locked the mating faces flush to ensure proper alignment and clearance.
